Frequently Asked Questions

What is GAFTIN?

GAFTIN (Global Accounting & Finance & Technology Innovation Network) is an independent professional networking and collaboration ecosystem for accounting, finance, AI and technology people.

Who can join GAFTIN?

Accounting firm and BPO owners, CPA/CA/CMA and tax professionals, AI accountants, FinTech and technology founders, analysts, and aspiring professionals.

Is membership free?

Yes. GAFTIN membership is free.

How can I apply?

Complete the form on the Apply for Membership page. It takes a few minutes.

Is every application reviewed?

Yes. The GAFTIN team reviews every application manually to keep the community trusted and professional.

How long does approval take?

Review times vary with application volume. The team will contact you by email or WhatsApp once your application has been reviewed.

How can I join the WhatsApp Community?

After approval, the team sends you a private invitation link. Joining is always your choice.

How can I join the Discord Server?

Approved members receive a private Discord invitation link with their approval message.

Can aspiring professionals apply?

Yes. Students and aspiring AI accountants or entrepreneurs are welcome.

Can international members join?

Yes. GAFTIN is a global network and welcomes applicants from every country.

Privacy Policy

We collect the details you submit in the membership form (name, email, optional WhatsApp number, country, professional details and your answers) only to review your application and contact you about membership. Data is stored in a restricted Google Sheet accessible only to GAFTIN administrators. We do not sell your data. We contact you by email, or WhatsApp only if you provided a number. To access or delete your data, contact the GAFTIN team.

Terms of Service

Membership is free and granted at the sole discretion of the GAFTIN team after manual review. Information you submit must be accurate. GAFTIN may remove members who breach these terms or the Code of Conduct. Community content reflects members' own views; GAFTIN does not provide professional, legal, tax or investment advice.

Community Code of Conduct

Be professional and respectful. Contribute knowledge and opportunities in good faith. No spam, unsolicited selling, harassment, discrimination or misleading claims. Do not share other members' private information or private invitation links. Confidential client information must never be posted.
